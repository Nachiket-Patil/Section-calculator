Engineering Application Notes

  • 🔹 Standard sections: Properties are exact values from IS:808 database
  • 🔹 Built-up sections: Calculations use rectangular approximation ignoring fillets, tapers, and welds
  • 🔹 Reinforcements: Assumes perfect bonding between components
  • 🔹 Output values: Provide conservative estimates for preliminary design
  • 🔹 Design application: For final structural design, apply appropriate safety factors and verify with detailed FEA

Note: This tool approximates compound section properties using first principles of mechanics. Actual performance may vary based on manufacturing tolerances, connection details, and material properties.
